Vidyagiri, Bagalkot

A new parlour with an old idea

Old Mumbai is a name families have trusted for ice cream made with milk, not shortcuts. When we opened our Bagalkot shop in Vidyagiri about two years ago, we wanted the same thing for our neighbours: a clean place to sit and a scoop you can feel good about.

That is why our menu leans on flavours you grew up with. Gajar Halwa from winter weddings, Kala Jamun from the fruit stall, Gulkand from your grandmother's cupboard. Come in, ask for a taste, and tell us what we should make next.

  • Pure milk and real fruits
  • No palm oil, no artificial preservatives
  • Indian flavours you will not find elsewhere in Bagalkot
  • Clean, bright seating for families
  • UPI QR payment accepted
